03 / My Journey

From a small place in Assam to building my future in Bengaluru.

My career was not built from a perfect roadmap. I learned by doing, by making mistakes, by taking responsibility, and by continuously teaching myself what I needed to know next.

01
THE BEGINNING

I didn't start with a privileged career path.

I come from Jagun, Margherita in Assam — a place far away from India's major corporate and technology hubs. There was no predefined path telling me exactly what I should become or how I should get there.

What I did have was the willingness to learn, work hard, adapt and keep moving forward.

01
THE FIRST STEP

Starting from customer service

My professional journey started at the ground level. Customer service taught me something that no classroom could teach me — how to listen, how to understand people, how to remain calm under pressure and how to solve problems when the customer is already frustrated.

I learned to communicate with different kinds of people, understand processes and take ownership instead of simply passing a problem to someone else.

Customer Service Communication Problem Solving Ownership
02
GROWING THROUGH EXPERIENCE

From handling customers to understanding the business

With experience, I started looking beyond individual customer issues. I wanted to understand why problems were happening, how processes could be improved and how customer experience connected directly to business performance.

My responsibilities gradually expanded into senior customer experience, VIP account management, retention, escalation handling and relationship management.

Customer Experience VIP Management Retention Account Management
03
THE BUSINESS SIDE

Learning that customers and revenue are connected

I moved deeper into business development and sales, where the responsibility became bigger. Customer relationships were no longer only about solving problems — they were also about creating value, retaining customers, developing accounts and contributing to revenue.

I learned how customer behaviour, engagement, retention, conversion and revenue are connected. That experience changed the way I think about business.

Business Development Sales Revenue Conversion Retention
04
LEADERSHIP

Becoming responsible for people, not just myself

Eventually, my role evolved into team leadership. Managing a team taught me a completely different lesson: individual performance and team performance are not the same thing.

I became responsible for coaching people, training new employees, monitoring performance, setting expectations, solving operational problems and helping team members improve.

Leading a team of people taught me that leadership is not about giving instructions. It is about creating clarity, building confidence, taking responsibility and helping other people perform better.

Team Leadership Coaching Training Performance People Management
05
THE BIG MOVE

Taking my career to Bengaluru

Moving from Assam to Bengaluru was more than changing cities. It meant stepping into a much larger professional ecosystem, surrounded by technology companies, startups, ambitious professionals and people constantly building something new.

Bengaluru gave me exposure to a different scale of opportunity. It also pushed me to think bigger about what I could learn, what I could build and what kind of career I wanted next.

Assam Bengaluru
06
SELF-TAUGHT TECHNOLOGY

I decided I didn't want to stop learning.

My professional background was primarily in customer experience, sales, business development and operations. But I became increasingly interested in technology and AI.

So I started teaching myself. Not because I had a computer science background, but because I wanted to understand how the technology behind the products I use actually works.

Step by step, I started working with Python, APIs, FastAPI, React, JavaScript, GitHub, local AI models, automation, speech recognition and AI agents.

I learned with documentation, experimentation, countless errors and practical projects — including building my own AI systems and data applications.

Python AI FastAPI React Automation LLMs
07
TODAY

Still learning. Still building. Still moving forward.

Today, I bring together the different parts of my journey: customer experience, business development, sales, people leadership, operations and technology.

I can understand a customer problem, think about the business impact, work with a team to solve it and increasingly build technology myself to make the solution better.

I am not trying to pretend that I started as a programmer, entrepreneur or technology expert. I didn't.

I am someone who kept learning until I could build things that I once thought were beyond my reach.

WHAT I BELIEVE

Your starting point doesn't have to determine your destination.

I started far away from India's biggest corporate centres. I learned through work, through mistakes, through people, through technology and through persistence. The journey from Assam to Bengaluru is not the end of my story. It is proof that I can keep learning, adapting and building whatever comes next.
